CONTACT IMMINENT v0.4.6.3 — NOTIFICATIONS + COMPROMISE AUDIT + BASE DEGRADATION
Parent: v0.4.6.2.

UI:
- Passive operational notifications now stack in the lower-left and fade automatically.
- Notifications remain color coded: hostile red, friendly blue, neutral green, intel gold.
- ACTIVE HOSTILE COLLECTION WITHIN AO persistent banner moved to bottom-center.
- Actionable Intel Drop choice panel remains separate from passive notifications.
- BLU-BASE renamed BLU-FOB throughout map, HUD, proximity metadata, and reporting.

Compromise audit:
- Live OE COMPROMISE, objective tracker, mission failure threshold, and result screen all use campaignCompromise in CONTESTED.
- Site exposure is intentionally separate from AO compromise: recovery actions can reduce AO compromise, but do not erase physical/site exposure already accumulated.
- Corrected 01-03/01-04 proximity site coordinates to the actual rendered BLUFOR positions.

Base degradation:
- Each BLUFOR site now maintains explicit exposure and degraded state.
- Hostile collection raises both AO compromise and exposure on the specific targeted site.
- Site exposure accumulates at 4x its accepted AO compromise contribution.
- At 100% site exposure the site becomes DEGRADED.
- All unresolved tracks stagnating/collecting around that fully exposed site are released from stationary behavior, stop collection there, and mobilize outward with randomized headings/speeds.
- Degraded sites are visibly marked in the BLUFOR STATUS panel.
- Future tracks tied to a degraded site are also released rather than allowed to stagnate there.
- Base degradation does not itself add extra AO compromise beyond the existing collection economy.

Balance retained:
- 0.12%/sec per hostile collector, 10% per-collector AO contribution cap, 0.25%/sec AO-wide ceiling.
- FRI -5%, NTL -3%, active collector disruption -2%, Intel Drop mechanics unchanged.
