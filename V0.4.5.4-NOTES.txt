CONTACT IMMINENT v0.4.5.4 — FRIENDLY & NEUTRAL EFFECTS
Parent: v0.4.5.3 CONTESTED Balance + Intel Drop.

New CONTESTED recovery mechanics:
- Correctly identify and CLEAR FRIENDLY: AO compromise -5 percentage points.
- Correctly identify and CLEAR NEUTRAL: AO compromise -3 percentage points.
- Compromise cannot fall below 0%.
- Effect occurs only on the normal successful disposition of that contact; resolved contacts cannot be farmed repeatedly.
- Visible operational notices:
    FRIENDLY PRESENCE CONFIRMED // AO COMPROMISE -X%
    NEUTRAL REPORTING CORROBORATED // AO COMPROMISE -X%
- Event log records the actual amount removed (important when compromise is below 5%/3%).

Unchanged:
- Hostile collection 0.20%/sec per active collector.
- Maximum 15% contribution per hostile/irregular collector.
- 60% AO compromise failure threshold.
- Intel Drop: MITIGATE -10% or EXPLOIT ISR Fusion +30% / 20 sec.
- Existing persistent SVG architecture, ISR movement, imagery and stall diagnostic.
