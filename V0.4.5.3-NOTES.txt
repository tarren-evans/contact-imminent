CONTACT IMMINENT v0.4.5.3 — CONTESTED BALANCE + INTEL DROP
Parent: v0.4.5.2 diagnostic hotfix.

CONTESTED balance:
- Hostile collection rate reduced from 0.55%/sec to 0.20%/sec per active collector.
- Each hostile/irregular collector can contribute a maximum of 15% AO compromise.
- Multiple simultaneous collectors remain additive.
- 60% AO compromise failure threshold unchanged.
- Existing 5-second dwell-to-collection behavior retained.

INTEL DROP:
- One guaranteed report arrives about 65 seconds after CONTESTED begins.
- Player has 10 seconds to choose:
  MITIGATE: immediately reduce AO compromise by 10 percentage points, floor 0%.
  EXPLOIT: activate INTEL FUSION for 20 seconds.
- INTEL FUSION expands effective ISR collection radius by 30%.
- Where the existing identification timer exposes the standard 1000ms threshold,
  fusion reduces it to 600ms during the buff.
- Intel Drop and fusion timers compensate correctly across pause/resume.
- No selection causes INTEL DROP EXPIRED.
- No new imagery.

Diagnostic stall guard from v0.4.5.2 retained unchanged.
